Bills Are Issued by SNGPL and SSGC

Gas bills are issued by Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited (SNGPL) and Sui Southern Gas Company (SSGC). The final amount due, the due date, the meter reading, and all charges are the ones printed on your official bill. If anything on this site differs from your official bill, the official bill is correct.

Calculator Results Are Estimates

The gas bill calculator on this site gives estimates only. Its figures are not officially verified billing. Tariffs, slabs, and adjustments can change the official figure, so always confirm the final amount on your official bill.
