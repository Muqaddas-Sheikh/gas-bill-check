About This Site

This is an independent consumer guide to gas bills in Pakistan. It was written to explain, in plain language, how to check a bill, understand its sections, estimate a future bill, and report a problem. It is not SNGPL, it is not SSGC, and it is not the government, and it does not speak for any of them.

What This Site Does

  • Explains which company supplies your area: SNGPL for Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad and Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and SSGC for Sindh and Balochistan.
  • Shows how to find and check the length of your bill number: 11 digits for SNGPL and 10 digits for SSGC.
  • Opens the official portal for your company, where your bill is actually displayed.
  • Explains bill sections, charges, protected and non-protected categories, duplicate bills, payments, new connections and complaints, in separate short guides.
  • Offers a calculator that estimates a bill from units you enter, clearly labelled as an estimate only.

What This Site Does Not Do

  • It does not generate, issue or change any gas bill.
  • It does not retrieve or display your bill itself.
  • It does not accept payments or change meter readings.
  • It does not open, close or transfer a gas connection.
  • It does not store your consumer number or any bill data.

Those actions happen only through the official SNGPL and SSGC channels. If a guide here and an official notice disagree, the official notice is the one to follow, and the amount on your official bill is always the final amount.

Why an Independent Guide Helps

Official portals are built to display bills, not always to explain them. Many readers arrive with a simple question — which company, which number, why is the amount different this month — and a short, plain answer saves a wasted trip or a confused phone call. That is the gap this site tries to fill: clear steps first, and a pointer to the official channel whenever an official action is needed.

The guides are written in original words for this site. Where a figure could change, such as a tariff or a price, the guide says so rather than printing a figure that may already be out of date. For urgent matters, especially a suspected gas leak, the guides direct readers to emergency action and the official helpline 1199 without delay.
