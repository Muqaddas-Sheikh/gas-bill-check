For Billing, Connection or Emergency Matters

This is an independent guide site, not SNGPL, SSGC or the government, so it cannot change a bill, fix a meter, open a connection or take a payment. For any of those matters, use the official channels:

  • Gas helpline: 1199.
  • Your company's official website and official complaint channel, reached through the home gas bill checker or the SNGPL and SSGC pages.
  • The guides on online complaints and gas leakage emergencies for step-by-step help.

If you smell gas, do not wait for an email reply from anyone: leave the area, avoid switches and flames, and follow the emergency guide at once.
