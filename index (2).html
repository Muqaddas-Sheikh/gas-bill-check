How to Check Your Sui Gas Bill Online

  1. Select your company — SNGPL if you are in Punjab, KPK, Islamabad or AJK; SSGC if you are in Sindh or Balochistan.
  2. Find your consumer number — 11 digits (Account ID) for SNGPL or 10 digits (Customer Number) for SSGC, printed on any old bill.
  3. Open the official portal — use the checker above; the official billing page opens in a new tab.
  4. View, download or print — use your browser's print option to save the duplicate bill as PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my SNGPL gas bill online?

Select SNGPL in the checker above, enter your 11-digit Consumer Number (Account ID on your old bill) and open the official SNGPL portal to view or print your duplicate bill.

How do I check my SSGC gas bill online?

Select SSGC, enter your 10-digit Customer Number and open the official SSGC view-bill portal to see your latest bill.

Is checking my gas bill online free?

Yes — viewing your bill on the official SNGPL or SSGC websites is completely free. You only pay the bill amount itself.

Why is my winter gas bill so high?

Heaters and geysers push usage into higher slabs where per-unit rates jump sharply. Try our gas bill calculator to estimate your slab.
