Submit Gas Meter Reading Online

If your gas bill is based on an estimated reading, your own meter reading can fix it. This guide explains how to submit your SNGPL gas meter reading online with the official SNGPL app, how to read the meter correctly, and what to do if the next bill still shows an estimated reading.

Why Submit Your Own Meter Reading?

A meter reader cannot always reach your meter. When that happens, the company may bill you on an estimated reading instead of the real one. An estimate can be too high or too low. If it is too high, you pay for gas you did not use that month. If it is too low, the extra units show up later, sometimes in a higher slab. Your own reading, sent on time with a clear photo, gives the company the real figure to bill from.

Keep your own note too. Write down the reading and the date, and keep a photo of the meter. If the bill later shows a different reading, that dated note is your proof.

How to Read Your Gas Meter

Read the meter safely, without opening any cover or touching pipes or fittings. The display shows black digits and red digits. The black digits are the main reading in hm³ (units). The red digits are the small fraction after the point. For your bill, use the black digits as they appear, left to right.

Take the photo in clear light, with no glare on the glass. The digits and the meter number should both be sharp in the picture. If the display is dirty, wipe only the outside glass gently. Do not force a stuck display and do not open the meter.

Submit Your Reading in the SNGPL App

SNGPL's official app is called SNGPL ConnectOn, published by Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited on Google Play and the App Store. Download it only from the official store and check the publisher name before installing. Do not download an APK file from a random website.

  1. Open the app and register or log in with your mobile number and your 11-digit SNGPL consumer number (Account ID on your old bill).
  2. Open the Self Meter Reading module from the dashboard.
  3. Take a clear photo of your meter with the app camera, so the digits are easy to read.
  4. Type the exact digits shown on the display into the reading field.
  5. Tap Submit Reading and save the reference ID or screenshot of the confirmation.

App screens and module names can change, so follow the steps shown inside the app. If a step looks different from this guide, use the app's own wording.

What If the Bill Still Shows an Estimated Reading?

First, check the dates. A reading sent after the billing date may apply to the next bill, not the one already printed. Compare the present reading on the bill with the reading you submitted and the date you sent it.

If the next bill still shows an estimate, call the helpline 1199 with your consumer number, the reading you submitted, the date, and the reference ID or screenshot. Ask for a complaint reference number and note the date. The estimated bill and wrong reading page explains this in more detail.

SSGC Customers

This guide covers SNGPL, which serves Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad and Azad Jammu and Kashmir. SSGC serves Sindh and Balochistan with a 10-digit customer number. No equivalent SSGC self-reading step was verified for this page, so SSGC customers should check the SSGC app or website, or call helpline 1199, before submitting a reading.

Common Mistakes

  • Reading the red digits as the main reading: Use the black digits for the main reading.
  • Typing the meter number instead of the reading: The meter number identifies the meter. The reading is the figure on its display.
  • A blurry photo: If the digits are not sharp, the reading may be rejected. Retake it in better light.
  • Sending it late: Send the reading on your reading day, before the bill is made, so it can be used for that month.

For a wrong reading on a bill you already have, the meter problem page explains how to report it. To check the bill itself, use the SNGPL bill check page with your 11-digit consumer number.
