By using this website, you agree to these terms. This site is an independent consumer guide for checking SNGPL and SSGC gas bills in Pakistan. It is not owned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by SNGPL, SSGC, or the Government of Pakistan.

What This Site Does

This site helps you find whether your supplier is SNGPL or SSGC, check the correct length of your bill number, open the official portal for your company, understand the sections and charges on a gas bill, and estimate a future bill from units or a meter reading. Its guides explain how to get a duplicate bill, pay, or report a problem, step by step.

What This Site Does Not Do

This site does not retrieve or display your bill, accept payments, change meter readings, or alter any official record. Those actions happen only through the official SNGPL or SSGC channels. The checker on this site only validates the length of the number you enter and opens the official portal.

Accuracy of Information

The information on this site is for general guidance only. Tariffs, slabs, charges, and company rules can change. We try to keep the guides correct, but we do not guarantee that every detail is complete or up to date. Always confirm amounts, due dates, and meter readings on your official bill before acting on them. We are not responsible for decisions you make based on this site's content.

Use of the Calculator

The gas bill calculator gives estimates only. Its figures are not officially verified billing and are not a substitute for the official bill. The final amount is always the one printed on your official bill.
