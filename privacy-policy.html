Privacy Policy

This page explains, in general terms, what this independent gas bill guide does and does not collect. In short: the checker on this site does not store your consumer number, and this site collects no bill data from you.

What the Checker Does with Your Number

When you use the checker on the home page, the number you type is checked in your own browser only, to confirm its length: 11 digits for SNGPL and 10 digits for SSGC. The number is not sent to this site, not saved by this site, and not stored in any database belonging to this site. After the length check, the checker opens the official SNGPL or SSGC portal, where you enter your details on that company's own website, under that company's own privacy rules.

What This Site Does Not Collect

  • No consumer numbers, account IDs or customer numbers.
  • No bill amounts, meter readings, due dates or payment details.
  • No names or addresses from bill checking or from the calculator.

The calculator likewise works in your browser from the figures you type. Nothing you enter into it is sent to or stored by this site.

Cookies and Advertising, in General Terms

Like many websites, this site may in future use basic cookies or display advertising through a third-party service. Any such service would operate under its own privacy policy, and its cookies or adverts would be governed by that provider's terms, not by this page alone. If advertising or analytics are added later, this page should be updated to name the services used and to explain how a reader can manage them. Until then, no specific advertising or analytics provider is claimed here.

Links to Other Websites

This site links to the official SNGPL and SSGC portals and to its own guide pages. Once you leave this site, the privacy rules of the website you visit apply instead. Please read the privacy policy of the official portal before entering personal or account details there.

Keeping Your Details Safe

Even though this site does not store your number, keep it private elsewhere too. Do not post a consumer or customer number on social media, and do not share it with strangers offering to check a bill for you. Share it only on the official company portal or through an official company channel, such as helpline 1199.

Useful Tools on This Site

Links to home gas bill checker, gas bill calculator, SNGPL bill check online and SSGC bill check online.

Important: the checker on this site validates the length of the number you enter and opens the official SNGPL or SSGC portal. It does not retrieve or display your bill itself. Calculator figures are estimates only, not officially verified billing, so always confirm the final amount on your official bill.
