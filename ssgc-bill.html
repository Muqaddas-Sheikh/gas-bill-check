How to Check Your SSGC Bill Online — Step by Step

  1. Find your 10-digit Customer Number on any previous SSGC bill.
  2. Enter it in the checker above and press Check SSGC Bill.
  3. The official SSGC view-bill portal opens — enter your number there to view the current bill.
  4. Use Print → Save as PDF to download your duplicate bill for EasyPaisa, JazzCash or bank payment.

SSGC Service Areas

SSGC (Sui Southern Gas Company) serves Sindh and Balochistan — including Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur and Quetta. If you are in Punjab, KPK or Islamabad, use our SNGPL bill check page instead.

SSGC Bill FAQs

What is my SSGC customer number?

It is the 10-digit Customer Number printed on your old SSGC bill.

How can I download my SSGC duplicate bill?

After your bill opens on the official portal, press Ctrl+P (or Share → Print on mobile) and choose "Save as PDF".

Can I pay my SSGC bill online?

Yes — after viewing the bill, note the amount and pay via your bank app, EasyPaisa or JazzCash using the customer number.
