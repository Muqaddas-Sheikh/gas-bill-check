SNGPL Bill History Online

Your old SNGPL bills tell you what you paid, when you paid, and how much gas you used. This guide shows you where to find your old bills online, what the SNGPL website shows, and what to do if an old bill is missing.

What Is SNGPL Bill History?

Bill history means your old bills, one after the other. Each old bill shows the month, the amount, the due date, the meter reading, and the units you used. Your latest bill and your old bills are two different things. The SNGPL website shows your latest bill. Your old bills are in the official SNGPL app, after you register once.

Check Old Bills in the SNGPL ConnectOn App

SNGPL's official app is called SNGPL ConnectOn. It is on Google Play and the App Store, published by Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited. Download it only from the official store and check the publisher name first. Do not download an APK file from a random website.

  1. Download SNGPL ConnectOn and open it.
  2. Register once with your 11-digit consumer number (Account ID on your old bill), the CNIC of the person whose name the connection is in, and a mobile number that can get SMS messages.
  3. After you register, open the bill history option. It may say History, Bill History, or words like that. The app screens can change, so follow the words you see inside the app.
  4. Pick the month you want to see. Check the month and year before you open it.
  5. Open the bill to see the amount, due date, readings, and units for that time. Save or download a copy if you need it.

Some guides say the app shows up to 12 months of old bills and whether you paid. That detail comes from other websites, not from SNGPL itself, so do not take it as a promise. If your app shows fewer months, that is normal.

What the SNGPL Website Shows

The SNGPL website is the fastest way to see your latest bill. Open the official SNGPL website, go to the bill section (Get Your Gas Bill), and type your 11-digit consumer number. The bill you see is the latest one, not a list of old bills.

Save a copy while you are there. In your browser, use Print and pick Save as PDF. That saved copy is a valid duplicate bill. Banks accept it, and you can pay with it through JazzCash and Easypaisa. If you save each bill when it comes, you will always have your own history and will never have to look for an old bill.

What to Check in an Old Bill

When you open an old bill, look at these things:

  • Billing month and period: make sure it is the month you wanted.
  • Total amount and due date: what you had to pay, and by when.
  • Meter readings and units: the old reading, the new reading, and how many units you used.
  • Arrears: money left unpaid from an old bill.
  • Payment status: whether the bill was paid.

If a record does not match your own receipt, keep both and call SNGPL. The bill charges page explains what each line on the bill means.

SNGPL Bills Come Every Two Months

SNGPL sends a bill every two months. One bill covers two months of gas use, and the meter reader comes every two months to read the meter. So in your history, each bill is for two months, not one. When you compare two bills, remember each one covers two months of use.

If an Old Bill Is Not Available Online

First, look for a copy you saved. An old paper bill, a saved PDF, or a payment receipt from JazzCash, Easypaisa, or your bank all work as proof.

If you have nothing saved, call the helpline 1199 with your consumer number, CNIC, and home address ready. You can also go to your nearest SNGPL customer service centre with the CNIC of the person whose name the connection is in. The office can print a bill for you on the spot. The SNGPL regional offices page lists the offices and their phone numbers.

SSGC Customers

This guide is for SNGPL, which serves Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad, and Azad Jammu and Kashmir. SSGC serves Sindh and Balochistan with a 10-digit customer number. SSGC customers can see current and old bills in the official SSGC Customer Connect app. The SSGC website shows the current bill. For help, call helpline 1199.

To check the bill itself, use the SNGPL bill check page with your 11-digit consumer number, or the duplicate bill page if you need a copy. If a bill shows a wrong reading, the estimated bill and wrong reading page explains what to do. To send your own reading, see the meter reading submit guide.
