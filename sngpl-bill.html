How to Check Your SNGPL Bill Online — Step by Step

  1. Find your 11-digit Consumer Number (printed as "Account ID" on any previous SNGPL bill).
  2. Enter it in the checker above and press Check SNGPL Bill.
  3. The official SNGPL bill portal opens — enter your number there to view the current bill.
  4. Use Print → Save as PDF to download your duplicate bill, or print it for bank payment.

SNGPL Service Areas

SNGPL (Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited) serves Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad and Azad Jammu & Kashmir — including Lahore, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Gujranwala and Sialkot. If you are in Sindh or Balochistan, use our SSGC bill check page instead.

SNGPL Bill FAQs

What is my SNGPL consumer number?

It is the 11-digit Account ID printed on your old SNGPL bill. Without it you cannot check your bill online.

How can I download my SNGPL duplicate bill?

After your bill opens on the official portal, press Ctrl+P (or Share → Print on mobile) and choose "Save as PDF".

Why is my SNGPL bill so high in winter?

Heaters push consumption into higher slabs. Estimate your bill with our gas bill calculator to see your slab.
