Which Company Supplies Your Gas?

Pakistan has two main piped-gas suppliers, and the right portal depends on where you live. Choosing the wrong company is the most common reason a bill search fails, so start here before you type anything.

SNGPL

Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited serves Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad and Azad Jammu and Kashmir. Its bill number, often called the Consumer Number or Account ID, has 11 digits.

SNGPL bill check online

SSGC

Sui Southern Gas Company serves Sindh and Balochistan. Its bill number, often called the Customer Number, has 10 digits.

SSGC bill check online

If you are unsure, look at the top of a previous printed bill. The company name is printed there, and the number of digits will confirm it for you.

How to Check Your Bill, Step by Step

  1. Find your gas company from your area or from an old bill.
  2. Copy your bill number exactly as printed, without spaces or dashes.
  3. Check the digit length: 11 digits for SNGPL and 10 digits for SSGC.
  4. Use the checker box above to open the official portal for your company.
  5. Enter your number on the official portal and view your bill there.
  6. Read the amount due, the due date and the meter reading, and save or print a copy for your records.

Keep the printed bill safe even after checking online. It is the easiest way to settle any later question about a reading, a charge or a payment.

If Your Bill Does Not Open

Do not panic if the official page shows no result on the first try. Work through this short checklist before trying again:

  • Confirm you selected the correct company for your area.
  • Count the digits again: 11 for SNGPL, 10 for SSGC.
  • Remove spaces, dashes and any extra characters from the number.
  • Check that you copied the number from the bill, not from a payment receipt or a different account.
  • Try a fresh browser tab, and check your internet connection.
  • If the portal itself seems down, wait a short while and try again later.
  • If the problem continues, contact your company through its official helpline 1199 or its official complaint channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an old bill to check online?

An old bill is the easiest source, because it shows both the company name and the full bill number. If you do not have one, check a previous payment record for the account details, or contact the company through its official channel to confirm your number.

Why does the checker ask for the company first?

Because the two companies use different number lengths and different portals. Selecting the company first avoids the common error of entering an 11-digit number on the 10-digit system, or the other way round.

Can this site show my current bill amount?

No. This site only validates the length of the number you type and opens the official portal. Your bill itself is shown only on the official SNGPL or SSGC website, after you enter your details there.

What should I do if my number has the wrong length?

Recheck the printed bill carefully. A missing or extra digit is the usual cause. If the printed number itself looks unclear, compare it with an older bill for the same connection before contacting the company.

How do I check my gas bill?

Confirm whether your supplier is SNGPL or SSGC, copy the consumer number from a printed bill, and use the checker above to open the official portal, where you enter the number again to view your bill.

Is the calculator result my final bill?

No. The calculator gives an estimate from the figures you enter. Tariffs, slabs and adjustments can change the official figure, so always confirm the final amount on your official bill.
