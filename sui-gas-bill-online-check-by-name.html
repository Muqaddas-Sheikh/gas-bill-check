Sui Gas Bill Online Check by Name

You cannot check your sui gas bill online by name alone. The official SNGPL and SSGC portals ask for your consumer number, not your name, because many people can share the same name. You need the number printed on an old bill to view your bill.

Why Name Alone Does Not Work

A name is not a unique identifier. Two neighbours can share a family name, and many people in the same city can have the same first and last name. The portal cannot tell which account you mean from a name alone. That is why it asks for the consumer number instead — one number belongs to one gas connection only, and it stays the same from month to month.

This is also a privacy rule. If bills could be checked by name, anyone who knew your name could view your bill and your gas usage. The consumer number keeps your account private: only people who have the number can view the bill.

What You Need Instead

You need the consumer number for your connection. For SNGPL (Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad and Azad Jammu and Kashmir), it is the 11-digit Consumer Number, sometimes called the Account ID. For SSGC (Sindh and Balochistan), it is the 10-digit Customer Number. Read our guide on sui gas bill online check by reference number to learn what this number is and where it sits on the bill.

How to Find Your Consumer Number

The easiest place to find the number is an old printed gas bill. It is printed near the top of the bill. If you do not have an old bill, check a previous payment record — a bank receipt, a JazzCash or Easypaisa payment record, or an SMS from a past payment often shows the number. If you still cannot find it, call the helpline 1199 and ask them to confirm your consumer number. Keep your CNIC and your address ready, because they will use them to confirm that the connection is yours.

How to Check Your Bill Once You Have the Number

Once you have the number, checking the bill takes a few minutes:

  • Open the official portal from our home gas bill checker.
  • Select your company: SNGPL bill check online for Punjab, KPK, Islamabad and AJK, or SSGC bill check online for Sindh and Balochistan.
  • Enter the number exactly as printed, without spaces or dashes.
  • View your current bill, the amount due, and the due date on the official portal.

Keep a copy of your bill or a photo of the number somewhere safe. Next month you will need the same number again, and having it saved means you will not have to search for an old bill every time.
