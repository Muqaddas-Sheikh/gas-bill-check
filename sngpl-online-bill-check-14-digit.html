SNGPL Online Bill Check 14 Digit

Your SNGPL bill number has 11 digits, not 14. If you are searching for a 14-digit number, you are probably thinking of an electricity bill — those reference numbers have 14 digits. For SNGPL, you need the 11-digit Consumer Number printed on your gas bill.

Why People Search for a 14-Digit SNGPL Number

Electricity bills in Pakistan use a 14-digit reference number. Companies like LESCO, MEPCO, FESCO and IESCO all print a 14-digit number on the electricity bill, and people use it to check the bill online. Gas and electricity are different services run by different companies, but because both bills are checked online, people often expect the gas bill to work the same way. That is where the 14-digit idea comes from.

SNGPL does not use a 14-digit number. Its Consumer Number has 11 digits. If you enter a 14-digit electricity reference number on the SNGPL portal, it will not work, because the portal only accepts the 11-digit gas consumer number.

What Is the Real SNGPL Number?

The real number is the 11-digit Consumer Number, sometimes called the Account ID. It is printed on your SNGPL gas bill, usually near the top. It stays the same from month to month for the same connection. Every SNGPL connection — in Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad and Azad Jammu and Kashmir — has its own 11-digit number. Read our SNGPL bill check online guide for the full steps.

How to Check Your SNGPL Bill With the 11-Digit Number

Once you have the 11-digit number, checking the bill is simple:

  • Find the 11-digit Consumer Number on an old printed gas bill.
  • Open the official SNGPL portal from our home gas bill checker.
  • Enter the 11 digits exactly as printed, without spaces or dashes.
  • View your current bill, the amount due, and the due date on the official portal.

If you are not sure which number is the consumer number, read our guide on sui gas bill online check by reference number, which shows where the number sits on the bill.

What If Your Number Does Not Work?

First, count the digits. The number must have exactly 11 digits. If you counted 14, you are looking at an electricity bill, not a gas bill. Second, check that you copied the number from a gas bill and not from a payment receipt or an electricity bill. Third, remove any spaces or dashes. If the printed number itself looks unclear, compare it with an older bill for the same connection. If the problem continues, call the SNGPL helpline 1199 and ask them to confirm your consumer number.
