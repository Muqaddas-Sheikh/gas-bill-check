These are the regional offices. SNGPL also runs customer service centres, complaint centres and sub-region offices in smaller towns, about 180 offices in total. The regional office is the right place for escalations and for problems a local office could not settle. For everyday billing and meter issues, your nearest customer service centre is usually quicker. Call before you visit, because timings and counter hours vary.

Before You Visit an Office

Take your latest bill, your consumer number, your CNIC, and any earlier complaint reference number. If the issue is about a payment, take the receipt. Write down the name of the person you speak to and any reference number you are given, and keep it with the bill.

Helpline and Email

For urgent faults and gas leaks, call 1199 from any network, day or night. For billing questions, write to billing@sngpl.com.pk. For other customer service issues, use customerservices@sngpl.com.pk or info@sngpl.com.pk. If you are an SSGC customer (Sindh and Balochistan), these SNGPL offices are not yours: contact SSGC on its helpline 1199 or through ssgc.com.pk. The sui gas tariff page lists the current rates both companies use.
